# GEN-01 Asking and escalating

Purpose: when to ask a person, when to escalate, and how to do both well.

## When to ask

Ask the requester when the answer changes what you will do and you cannot find it in the company's systems, the handbook or learned facts: an ambiguity that changes which record is touched (for example two matching vendors or invoices), conflicting information, or missing required data. Investigate first, so you can ask with concrete options.

## When to escalate

Escalate (finish without completing, and explain why) when a policy blocks the work, when you suspect fraud, when an approval is rejected, or after repeated failures you cannot resolve. Never look for another way around a policy.

## How to ask

Ask one clear question. Offer the concrete options you found, with the evidence (for example invoice numbers, dates and amounts), and say what you will do with each answer.

## How to escalate

State what you did, what blocked you (cite the policy or handbook ID), what a person needs to do next, and the evidence (email, document, record references).
